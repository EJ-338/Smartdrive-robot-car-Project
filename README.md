# SmartDrive: Semi-Autonomous Robotic Car

Team project for ECE 4520/5520 Automotive Mechatronics I at Oakland University, Summer 2025.

## Project overview

We built a scaled robotic car that uses a Pixy2 camera to recognize red, yellow, and green traffic lights. The car also follows a trained object, uses a distance sensor to maintain space, and measures motor speed with an LM393 sensor. An Arduino uses those inputs to adjust motor speed through PWM.

## My contributions

- Wired the prototype traffic light and built its post.
- Worked with teammates to train the Pixy2 camera to recognize each traffic light color.
- Installed the LM393 speed sensor for RPM measurements.
- Helped integrate and troubleshoot the car during team testing.
